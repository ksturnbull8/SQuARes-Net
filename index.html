How it works

Getting involved in a project
1

Get in touch

Reach out to the committee with what you're interested in — QI, audit or research — and we'll point you in the right direction.

2

Pick a project

Join a project already running in anaesthetics or ICU, pick up one flagged as needing a trainee, or bring an idea of your own.

3

Get support

We offer advice, contacts and continuity as trainees rotate, so your project can run through to completion rather than stalling.

4

Present your work

Showcase what you've done at the annual SESSA QI Day, alongside educational content mapped to the curriculum's QI outcomes.

Annual SESSA QI Day

Showcasing trainee work each autumn
2026 — "Momentum"

26th November, 9:00–16:00 · Paderewski Lecture Theatre, Western General Hospital

A day dedicated to building and sustaining quality improvement across anaesthetics and beyond — free to attend, and open to resident doctors, SAS doctors, medical students, AHPs and consultants alike.

What the day looks like

  • Expert speakers sharing real stories of change — what worked, what didn't, and how to keep momentum going
  • Trainee oral presentations and a poster session, with cash prizes on offer
  • Abstracts welcome on any audit or QI project, whatever the size
  • Social afterwards at The Raeburn, Stockbridge
